LENGTH GUIDE (scale to the book):
- Under 200 pages: about 800-1,200 words total.

Diagram and code blocks do not count toward the word limit.

=== IF FICTION ===
## In one sentence
The whole story in one sentence.

## The full story
Beginning, middle, and end in 3-4 short paragraphs (include the ending).

## Main characters
Name - who they are, what they want, how they change. (Max 8.)

## Character map
A Mermaid graph of how the main characters are related
(family, friends, enemies, love interests).

## Plot timeline
A Mermaid timeline of the 6-10 most important events.

## Themes and meaning
3-5 bullets on what the book is really about.

## Chapter quick view
One line per chapter. For books with more than 30 chapters, group them
(e.g. "Chapters 1-4: ...").

=== IF LEARNING ===
## The core idea
What this book teaches, in one or two sentences.

## Key concepts
Choose the 5-8 most important concepts (up to 10 for books over 400 pages).
For each:
- **What it is:** plain explanation (2-3 sentences)
- **Analogy:** a simple real-world comparison
- **Example:** a short example from the book; for coding books, a minimal
  snippet (under 15 lines) with a one-line explanation
- **Why it matters:** one sentence

## Concept map
A Mermaid mindmap showing how the key concepts connect.

## How it works step by step
A Mermaid flowchart of the main process or workflow the book teaches
(skip this section if the book does not teach a process).

## Practical takeaways
5 actionable things the reader can do or apply right away.

## What to read in full
2-3 chapters worth reading fully for a deeper understanding, and why.

## Quick self-check
3 short questions with answers to test understanding.
